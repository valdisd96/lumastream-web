LumaStream / Questions & answers

Good questions. Honest answers.

What LumaStream does, and what you should not expect it to do.

Are movies included?

No. This is a player, not a subscription service. It includes no films, credentials or guaranteed sources. Only obtain and share content you are authorized to use.

Do I need an account or a separate server?

Not for direct magnets and torrents. The torrent engine runs on your Android device. Optional catalog and online subtitle integrations require their own setup; network playback still needs internet access.

Why won’t a source play?

Peers may be unavailable, the connection may be too slow, or your device may not support the codec or format. There is no transcoding. Try another source; automatic selection is not a playback guarantee.

Can I save films for offline viewing?

Not currently. The cache is temporary and clears on exit. Background downloads, background playback and picture-in-picture are not supported.

What does Continuous prefetch do?

When enabled, it fetches video ahead during playback, buffering and pause. It waits when the cache is full and refills as space is reclaimed. This is not offline saving and does not increase your cache limit.

Are subtitles supported?

Yes: supported embedded tracks, matching files in the torrent and SRT/WebVTT import. Online OpenSubtitles search needs separate application-provider setup, not included out of the box. Choose matches explicitly; they may be wrong.

Does my history sync across devices?

No. Settings, Watch later and history are local to each installation. Uninstalling deletes this data.

Is torrent traffic anonymous?

No. BitTorrent can upload to peers, who can see your network IP. Optional catalog, source and subtitle services receive requests when used.

Which devices have been tested?

A physical XGIMI Android TV projector and ARM64 Google TV, phone and tablet emulators. Physical phones/tablets and x86 runtime have not been verified. Website screenshots are from 0.8.0 checks, not a fresh certification of every device.

What about Internet Archive?

Search is available from Home. Review the item’s rights and Archive terms: collection access is restricted to scholarship and research purposes. A reported license alone is not legal clearance.

Still need a hand?

Include the app version, device, Android version, steps and what happened. Remove tokens, private URLs and personal information from logs and screenshots.
